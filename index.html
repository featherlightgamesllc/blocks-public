BreezeBlocks Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 September 2026
Publisher: Jia Spaces LLC ("we", "us")
Contact: feather.light.games.llc@gmail.com

BreezeBlocks is a calm falling-block puzzle game for Android and iOS. This policy explains what information the app handles, what stays on your device, and what leaves it. The short version: there is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, and nothing you type is ever sent to us. The app keeps your scores and settings on your device and sends anonymous usage statistics to Google's Firebase Analytics so we can see how the game is played.

1. Information stored on your device

The app keeps the following only in its own private storage on your phone or tablet:

  • Settings — your preferences such as theme, game mode, language, sound and accessibility options.
  • Custom modes — the names and piece sets of modes you compose.
  • Score history — the results of your past sessions, used for the local top-scores list.
  • Purchase status — whether the in-app purchase has been made, and the daily-preview state for free players.

This data never leaves the device through the app. It is deleted when you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Your operating system may include it in device backups you control (Google's Android backup, or Apple's iCloud or local backup).

2. Anonymous usage analytics

The Android and iOS apps use Google Analytics for Firebase to record how the game is used. We use this to understand which modes, themes and screens people use, where sessions are abandoned, and whether features work, so we can improve the game.

What is recorded. How the game is used: which screens, modes and themes are used, when sessions start, end or are abandoned and how they went (for example score, rows cleared and length), which settings change, and whether purchases and sharing succeed. Event values are catalog identifiers and numbers. We never record names, email addresses, custom-mode names, or any other text you enter.

What Google collects automatically with those events: a random app-instance identifier generated when the app is first opened, device model and operating-system version, app version, device language, and an approximate location (country or region) derived from the network address at the time of the event. Advertising identifiers are not collected, and the data is not used for advertising.

Who it identifies. Nobody. The app-instance identifier is a random number, is not linked to your name, email, phone number or store account, and is reset when you uninstall and reinstall the app.

How long it is kept. Google retains event data tied to the app-instance identifier for the retention period configured on our Analytics project; aggregated statistics are kept longer. Google processes this data under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. More on how Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

How to stop it. There is no account to delete because none exists. Uninstalling the app ends collection; reinstalling starts a new, unrelated identifier.

3. Purchases

BreezeBlocks offers optional in-app purchases. They are sold and processed entirely by Google Play (on Android) or the Apple App Store (on iOS). We never see or store your payment card, billing address or store password. The store gives us an order record (order number, product, price, currency and country) for accounting, taxes and refunds, and tells the app on your device whether the purchase is valid so it can unlock the content and restore it on your other devices signed into the same store account. Refunds are handled by the store under its terms.

4. Sharing a session card

After a session you can share an image of your results. The image is generated on your device and handed to the system share sheet only when you tap share. It goes only to the app or contact you choose; we do not receive it.

5. Children

BreezeBlocks is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. It contains no advertising, chat, social features or account creation, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Your rights and choices

Because the app holds no data that identifies you, we cannot look up, export or delete records for a specific person. What you can do:

  • Delete everything the app stores by clearing its storage or uninstalling it.
  • Stop analytics by uninstalling the app.
  • Ask us anything about this policy at the contact address above. Residents of the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, California and other places with privacy laws have rights of access, correction, deletion and objection; write to us and we will explain what applies given that we hold only anonymous statistics.

We do not sell personal information and do not share it with anyone other than the service providers named in this policy.

7. Service providers

  • Google LLC — Firebase Analytics (usage statistics) and Google Play Billing (purchases on Android).
  • Apple Inc. — App Store and StoreKit (purchases on iOS).

8. Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling information differently, we will update this page and the effective date at the top. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
